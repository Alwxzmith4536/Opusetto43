# ONE PUNCH BLOCK: Bald vs. Tornado
*A Minecraft animation story (fan parody of One Punch Man)*

**Runtime:** about 7–8 minutes
**Style:** Classic blocky Minecraft animation. Square sun, chunky particles, vanilla sound effects.

---

## Cast & Skins

| Character | Skin notes |
|---|---|
| **Saitama** | Yellow jumpsuit, white cape, red gloves. The head is a plain peach cube with two dot eyes. Running gag: it's the only *perfect* cube in the whole world. |
| **Tatsumaki** | Black dress with green trim, curly green hair as a 3D layer, a green glowing outline whenever she uses her powers. |
| **Genos** | Iron-golem arms, blond hair, glowing orange eyes. |
| **Clerk** | A Hero Association villager in a suit. Only ever says "Hmm." |

---

## SCENE 1 — City Z, Morning

**CAM:** Slow pan across the empty, cracked streets of City Z. A creeper hisses somewhere and nobody cares.

Saitama walks out of his apartment holding a paper item: **"HERO ASSOCIATION — NOW HIRING."**

> **GENOS:** Sensei! With your strength, you'll be S-Class by noon.
>
> **SAITAMA:** I just want people to know who I am. *(beat)* Also, the flyer says there's a free snack table.

**SFX:** *Eating crunch.* Saitama is already chewing a cookie.

---

## SCENE 2 — Hero Association HQ

**CAM:** Big reveal shot of the HQ, a huge quartz-and-obsidian tower with a beacon beam shooting out the top.

The registration desk. The **Clerk** villager looks Saitama up and down.

> **CLERK:** Hmm.
>
> **SAITAMA:** Uh... hi. Saitama. Here to sign up.
>
> **CLERK:** *(points at a punching machine, a target block on a piston)* Hmm.

Saitama taps it. One finger.

**SFX:** *Ding.*

The machine shoots straight back through **47 walls**, out of the building and into the sky. **CAM:** follow it up past the clouds until it's a single pixel. A small *twinkle*.

> **CLERK:** HMMMMM!!!

**SFX:** Bells ring all over the tower (village raid alarm).

---

## SCENE 3 — The Tornado Arrives

The lights flicker. Every loose block in the room starts floating: chairs, flower pots, the Clerk.

**CAM:** Low angle. **Tatsumaki** drifts down through the hole in the ceiling, glowing green, arms crossed.

> **TATSUMAKI:** Who's the boiled egg wrecking *my* headquarters?
>
> **SAITAMA:** I'm here to sign up.
>
> **TATSUMAKI:** *You?* With a cape? And that *head*? Go home, baldy.

She lifts him into the air with a flick of her finger and spins him like a top.

**CAM:** Close-up on Saitama spinning at full speed, completely blank-faced, picking his nose.

> **SAITAMA:** Can you put me down? The form's on the desk.
>
> **TATSUMAKI:** *(vein pops, a red pixel on her forehead)* ...Outside. **NOW.**

She throws him through the wall.

---

## SCENE 4 — The Battle

**Location:** A huge plains biome outside the city.

**ROUND 1 — The Mountain**
Tatsumaki rips a whole mountain out of the ground. A cow is still standing on top of it.

> **COW:** *Moo?*

She hurls it. Saitama gives it a light punch. The mountain turns into a perfectly even rain of cobblestone. The cow lands gently next to him.

> **SAITAMA:** *(to the cow)* You good?

**ROUND 2 — Anvil Storm**
The sky fills with **hundreds of anvils**.

**SFX:** *CLANG CLANG CLANG CLANG.*

Saitama walks through them like light rain. He catches the last one and sets it down neatly.

> **SAITAMA:** These are expensive, you know.

**ROUND 3 — Sandstorm**
She pulls up an entire desert. Tons of sand bury him. *Silence.*

> **TATSUMAKI:** *(panting)* Hah... finally...

One yellow glove pokes out of the sand dune. He climbs out and shakes sand out of his cape. One grain falls off his perfectly cube head.

**ROUND 4 — The Meteor**
Tatsumaki's outline goes from green to **blinding white**. She drags a giant **netherrack meteor** down from space, on fire and roaring.

> **TATSUMAKI:** THIS ONE YOU CAN'T STOP!

**CAM:** Music cuts out. Wide shot of the tiny yellow figure under the giant burning rock.

Saitama crouches. Jumps.

> **SAITAMA:** Normal... Punch.

**SFX:** *Pop.*

The meteor bursts into thousands of glowing **XP orbs** that rain down over the whole plain.

**SFX:** *ding-ding-ding-ding-ding*

The orbs pour onto Tatsumaki. **LEVEL UP** pops over her head.

> **TATSUMAKI:** ...Did you just give me *experience*?!

She drops to her knees, hunger bar empty, out of breath. Saitama walks over. She shuts her eyes, waiting for the hit.

He just gives her a little flick on the forehead.

> **SAITAMA:** Nice fight. Nobody's made me jump that high in a long time.
>
> **TATSUMAKI:** *(eyes wide)* ...You're not mad?
>
> **SAITAMA:** Why would I be mad? That was kind of fun.

---

## SCENE 5 — The Decision

The **Clerk** runs out holding a clipboard, followed by Genos.

> **CLERK:** Hmm. *(holds up a sign: DAMAGE — 1,204,377 BLOCKS)*
> **CLERK:** Hmm. *(second sign: FEE — 9,000 EMERALDS)*
> **CLERK:** Hmm. *(drops a stack of **64 Book & Quills** at his feet: APPLICATION FORM)*

Saitama looks at the stack. He looks at the clock item in his hand: **4:58 PM.**

> **SAITAMA:** ...The supermarket sale ends at five.

He puts the quill down.

> **SAITAMA:** Nah. I'll keep being a hero for fun.
>
> **TATSUMAKI:** You're NOT joining?! After all that?!
>
> **SAITAMA:** I don't need a rank to help people. *(points at the books)* Besides, that paperwork is scarier than you.
>
> **GENOS:** *(writing in his own book)* Sensei's wisdom knows no limit...

Saitama walks off into the orange afternoon light. Tatsumaki watches him go and doesn't say anything.

---

## SCENE 6 — After the Battle: Friends (Love Scene +++)

### Part 1: The Last Pack of Beef

**Location:** The supermarket. One pack of discount **raw beef** left on the shelf.

Saitama reaches for it. It floats away, glowing green, into Tatsumaki's hands.

> **TATSUMAKI:** Oh! What a *coincidence*. I wasn't following you or anything.
>
> **SAITAMA:** ...That's my beef.
>
> **TATSUMAKI:** *(looks away, then pushes it into his arms)* Hmph. Take it. It's a reward for... not dying.
>
> **SAITAMA:** Thanks, Tatsumaki.

**SFX:** a soft *pling*. A tiny pink pixel blush shows up on her face.

### Part 2: The Walk Home

**CAM:** Side-scroll shot. The two walk down the street. His grocery bags float behind them in a neat little line, glowing green.

> **SAITAMA:** You don't have to carry those.
>
> **TATSUMAKI:** I'm not *carrying* them. I'm *floating* them. Totally different.

A few **heart particles** pop over her head. She swats them away.

> **TATSUMAKI:** Those are NOT mine.

### Part 3: Rooftop Sunset

**Location:** The roof of Saitama's apartment. The square sun is going down and the sky goes from orange to purple.

They sit on the edge of the roof, legs hanging over, sharing a bowl of mushroom stew.

> **TATSUMAKI:** *(quietly)* Everyone either needs me to protect them or wants to stay away from me. I'm always the strongest one in the room, so I'm always on my own.
>
> **TATSUMAKI:** But today you just... stood there. You didn't break. And it felt like I could finally put something down.
>
> **SAITAMA:** *(looking at the sunset)* You're really strong, Tatsumaki. You don't have to fight everyone by yourself all the time.

**CAM:** Close-up on her face. Her green outline flickers softly, like a heartbeat.

> **TATSUMAKI:** ...So. Are we friends now, or what?
>
> **SAITAMA:** Yeah. Friends.

She slowly leans her head on his shoulder.

**CAM:** Slow push-in on their blocky hands on the roof. Her hand moves over and rests on his.

**SFX:** *Firework rocket launch... BOOM.* Red and gold fireworks light up the sky.

**CAM:** Cut to the bushes below. **Genos** is holding a stack of firework rockets.

> **GENOS:** *(whispering)* For the atmosphere, Sensei.

**CAM:** Back on the roof. In the firework light, Tatsumaki quickly kisses Saitama on the cheek.

**SFX:** *pop!* A big burst of heart particles.

She launches straight up into the sky like a green comet.

> **TATSUMAKI:** *(from far away)* D-DON'T READ INTO THAT, BALDY!!!

Saitama touches his cheek. For the first time all day, he smiles a little.

> **SAITAMA:** ...Huh.

**CAM:** Pull back. He looks around the roof.

> **SAITAMA:** ...Wait. She took my groceries.

**CAM:** Way off in the sky, a green streak flies away with a line of floating grocery bags behind it.

**CUT TO BLACK.**

---

## POST-CREDITS SCENE

**Next morning.** *Knock knock.* Saitama opens the door.

Tatsumaki is floating there with the grocery bags, arms crossed and looking away.

> **TATSUMAKI:** I'm not here to see you. I'm here to... inspect the building.
>
> **GENOS:** *(from the kitchen)* Sensei, should I make three bowls?
>
> **SAITAMA:** *(steps aside to let her in)* Yeah. Three.

Tatsumaki floats in. A single heart particle pops above her head. She doesn't swat this one away.

**END.**

---

## Production Notes

- **Music:** quiet, lazy slice-of-life music for Saitama's scenes, loud orchestral for the battle, soft piano on the rooftop.
- **Key Minecraft SFX:** villager "hmm", anvil landing, sand falling, XP orb ding, level-up chime, firework launch and burst.
- **Visual gags to keep:** Saitama's perfect-cube head, the cow surviving every round, Genos taking notes in a Book & Quill, heart particles over Tatsumaki that she keeps denying.
