# Seek POV — Part 4: The Garden

Part 4 of the fan-made indie-horror anime short. Senpai slams into Seek and pulls Nagatoro free;
Gamo-chan and Sakura run off for good. Seek punches through the hallway floor and everyone falls
into the school courtyard garden, where Nagatoro and Senpai hide in the tall grass while Seek
searches for them, seen first-person through Seek's eye.

- `seek-pov-part4.mp4` / `seek-pov-part4-android.mp4` — the 64 s video (1280×720, 24 fps, with sound)
- `index.html` — the scene (Three.js); `render.cjs` renders it, `make_audio.py` makes the soundtrack

Rebuild the same way as Part 1 (see `../seek-pov-part1/README.md`). Nagatoro and Seek belong to
their creators; non-commercial fan work, all assets made from scratch.
