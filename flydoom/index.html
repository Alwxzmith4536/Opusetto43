Eye to lobula. Each ommatidium raycasts the level. Lamina L2 cells sharpen dark-object contrast, LC11 cells detect small moving objects, LC4 cells detect looming, and lobula-plate tangential cells sense nearby walls from optic flow.

Innate reflexes. Hardwired lobula-to-descending-neuron paths turn the fly toward objects (DNa02 steering), away from walls, and back off through the giant fiber and MDN "moonwalker" neurons when something looms. The fly has no innate urge to shoot.

Learning. 300 Kenyon cells form a sparse code of the scene, kept sparse by APL inhibition. KC→MBON synapses change by a three-factor rule: Kenyon cell activity × efference copy of the chosen action × dopamine from PAM (reward) or PPL1 (punishment) cells.

Controls. The test engine compares this fly with the same brain frozen, with its innate wiring scrambled, with the mushroom body lesioned, and with random play. Run it with node flydoom/test-engine.js.
