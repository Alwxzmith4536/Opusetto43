# Seek POV — Part 3: The Drain

Part 3 of the fan-made indie-horror anime short. Nagatoro pokes at the floor drain, Seek bursts
out in a geyser of ink and grabs her, and his goo spreads over her school uniform with little eyes
opening in it. Senpai, Gamo-chan and Sakura run, and Seek chases them down the red-lit hallway
DOORS-style while Nagatoro cries, until Senpai stops and turns back for her.

- `seek-pov-part3.mp4` / `seek-pov-part3-android.mp4` — the 70 s video (1280×720, 24 fps, with sound)
- `index.html` — the scene (Three.js); `render.cjs` renders it, `make_audio.py` makes the soundtrack

Rebuild the same way as Part 1 (see `../seek-pov-part1/README.md`). Nagatoro and Seek belong to
their creators; non-commercial fan work, all assets made from scratch.
