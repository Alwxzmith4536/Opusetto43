# Seek POV — Final: The Long Galaxy

The last episode of the fan-made indie-horror anime short. Seek's eye turns red on Nagatoro and
Senpai and a wave of ink swallows the street (nothing is shown; the screen goes dark). Seek rises
over the city, leaves Earth behind, warps across 2.5 million light-years to a lonely spiral galaxy,
and goes supernova. Ends on THE END and the episode list.

- `seek-pov-final.mp4` / `seek-pov-final-android.mp4` — the 74 s video (1280×720, 24 fps, with sound)
- `index.html` — the scene (Three.js); `render.cjs` renders it, `make_audio.py` makes the soundtrack

Rebuild the same way as Part 1 (see `../seek-pov-part1/README.md`). Non-commercial fan work, all
assets made from scratch.
