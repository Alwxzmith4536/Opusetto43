# Across the Doors – Part 1: "The New Kid"

Indie 3D fan animation (Three.js, ~90 s). Gwen Stacy meets Miles Morales at school and they head to class, all seen
through a DOORS-style Seeker's eye (locker-vent peek, corner lean, door crack, under-desk peek, goo drips, eye blinks,
flickering lights, chunky low-res look).

Run: `cd gwen-miles-part1 && python3 -m http.server` then open http://localhost:8000 and press **Open the door**.
Use **Record .webm** to export the animation as a video.

Characters are original low-poly stand-ins. To use your own licensed models, drop `assets/gwen.glb` and
`assets/miles.glb` here; they are loaded automatically.
