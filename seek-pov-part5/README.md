# Seek POV — Part 5: After Dark

Part 5 of the fan-made indie-horror anime short, and a breather. Seek leaves the school, wanders
down a night street into Bar Kitsune, where a fox bartender is shaking cocktails. Seek gurgles
for a beer, she pours one from the tap and slides it over, and Seek chugs it, burps, and gets the
first one on the house. Seen first-person through Seek's eye.

- `seek-pov-part5.mp4` / `seek-pov-part5-android.mp4` — the 63 s video (1280×720, 24 fps, with sound)
- `index.html` — the scene (Three.js); `render.cjs` renders it, `make_audio.py` makes the soundtrack

Rebuild the same way as Part 1 (see `../seek-pov-part1/README.md`). Nagatoro and Seek belong to
their creators; the fox bartender is an original character. Non-commercial fan work, all assets
made from scratch.
