      The model is built from primitives in code, so there is nothing to download to view it. Node names (Hips, R_ForeArm, WheelBase, WheelSpin…) and the clips Idle, Walk, Slash, Hit and WheelTurn are kept in the export, so a game engine can play them directly. To turn the wheel in a game, subtract 45° from WheelBase's Z rotation and play WheelTurn. The export button works when this file is opened locally; a prebuilt mahoraga.glb sits next to it in the repo.
