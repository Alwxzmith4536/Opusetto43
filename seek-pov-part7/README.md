# Seek POV — Part 7: Seek's Revenge

Part 7 of the fan-made indie-horror anime short. Seek wakes up in the street with a banana peel on
its eye, powers up into golden Super Seek mode, vaporizes the fox bartender's banana barrage, and
knocks her out cold (cartoon K.O., swirly eyes) with a golden SEEK BEAM while Nagatoro and Senpai
watch from the doorway.

- `seek-pov-part7.mp4` / `seek-pov-part7-android.mp4` — the 66 s video (1280×720, 24 fps, with sound)
- `index.html` — the scene (Three.js); `render.cjs` renders it, `make_audio.py` makes the soundtrack

Rebuild the same way as Part 1 (see `../seek-pov-part1/README.md`). Non-commercial fan work, all
assets made from scratch.
