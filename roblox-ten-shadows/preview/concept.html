BEHAVIOUR (canon → game)

  • Adaptation: every 3 hits of a damage type turn the wheel one notch (−17% damage). 4 notches = adapted: 95% resist, heals 6%, shockwave.
  • Counter: using an adapted technique can make it blink behind you and strike.
  • Untamed ritual: summoned with Furube Yura Yura, it attacks everyone, summoner included.
  • Taming: defeat it and everyone who fought unlocks it as a summon (H).
  • Phase 2 (<50% HP): faster, shorter cooldowns, World-Cutting Slash once it has adapted to something.

STATS & FAIRNESS

  • 16,000 HP (+60% per extra player), takes 80% damage before adaptation.
  • Attacks: 3-hit combo, Sword of Extermination, leaping Ground Slam, Rush, World-Cutting Slash — all telegraphed in red.
  • No instant kills: one hit never removes more than 20% of max HP; above 35% HP a hit can't kill; 0.35 s i-frames after each hit; Guard (F) blocks 60%.
  • From full HP it takes at least 5 hits to fall (unit-tested).
