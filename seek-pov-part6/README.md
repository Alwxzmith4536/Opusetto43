# Seek POV — Part 6: Last Call

Part 6 of the fan-made indie-horror anime short. Nagatoro and Senpai walk into Bar Kitsune and find
Seek at the counter. The fox bartender realizes Seek is the monster that wrecked the school, pelts
it with bananas, then goes into nine-tailed battle mode (fox-fire orbs, glowing eyes, headband) for
a banana barrage and a MEGA BANANA that knocks Seek out into the street.

- `seek-pov-part6.mp4` / `seek-pov-part6-android.mp4` — the 68 s video (1280×720, 24 fps, with sound)
- `index.html` — the scene (Three.js); `render.cjs` renders it, `make_audio.py` makes the soundtrack

Rebuild the same way as Part 1 (see `../seek-pov-part1/README.md`). Non-commercial fan work, all
assets made from scratch.
